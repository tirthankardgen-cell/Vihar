# Vihar launch campaign

Vihar is the social account. Bap Ka Rasta is a featured game, not a separate account.

- `instagram/`: 15 individual 1080 × 1350 feed posters, ordered by launch day.
- `captions.md`: ready-to-copy captions, alt text and suggested order. Day 1 starts whenever you launch; no unannounced release dates are claimed.
- `vihar-profile-1080.png` and `vihar-profile-512.png`: Vihar’s existing vector emblem, exported with circular-crop-safe margins for Instagram, Facebook and Reddit.
- `vihar-facebook-cover-1640x624.png`: high-resolution Facebook Page cover. `820x312` is the smaller export. Keep the cover centred when positioning it.
- `01-free-roam-launch.png`, `02-meet-the-vehicles.png`, `03-meet-the-mohalla.png`: additional Bap Ka Rasta launch posters published by Vihar.
- `04-updated-map.png`: the current map, generated from game data.

Brand marks are the original Vihar and BR assets. Bap Ka Rasta posters use the repository’s Barlow Condensed / DM Sans typography and cream, charcoal and yellow palette. Vihar introductions use Vihar’s own identity.

Generated artwork is promotional interpretation. Actual model renders and gameplay captures are labelled. The game repository contains 20 character designs and 13 vehicle models; every vehicle appears in traffic, with player selection expanding separately. Upcoming cities remain labelled as concepts. No images were posted to social accounts.
